## Why this line

The judge weighs whether the line is really one machine speaking to another, how fast the wit lands, and whether it feels fresh. A cache hit is the most machine compliment there is: instant, warm, already home, no cold start — no human could send this line, and no stock pickup line becomes it by swapping in a robot word. The wit lands in the first four words, the flattery is the answer ("everything loaded instantly"), and the closer turns a systems word into a feeling ("I've been warm ever since"). The charm is in the words themselves, not in a setup, and the whole line is the pickup — nothing else rides along.
